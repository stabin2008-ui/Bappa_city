FESTIVAL GUIDE

🏃

Controls

Desktop: Left / Right Arrow or A / D to switch lanes.
Space: Activate Bappa's Blessing.
P: Pause game.
Mobile: Tap LEFT, RIGHT & POWER buttons.

🍯

Modaks & Combos

Collect golden Modaks to increase score and build combo multipliers up to 5x. Modaks also charge Bappa's Blessing meter!

🚧

Obstacles & Safety

Dodge barricades, dhol drums, flower crates, and carts. Collisions damage safety and break combos. Fair collision boxes give clean reaction space.

🤝

Festival Events

Encounter lost children, blocked processions, and fallen decorations. Your quick moral choices determine Festival Safety and Reputation!

🌅

Day to Night

Journey from peaceful morning to vibrant sunset, dusky evening, and celebratory midnight lit with thousands of diyas.

🌊

Visarjan Finale

Travel 2800 meters to reach the sacred ghat for the grand Visarjan celebration with fireworks and floating diyas!
